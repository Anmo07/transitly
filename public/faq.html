How Can We Help You?

Everything you need to know about intercity bus parcel booking, live GPS tracking, rate cards, and delivery handovers.

search

Transitly monetizes scheduled public transport vehicle cargo holds (such as state roadways buses operating between Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, and Rajasthan). When you book, your shipment is paired with an upcoming departure on that highway corridor. Doorstep courier partners (Uber Direct, Rapido) collect the package from your door, transport it to the origin ISBT terminal, and upon arrival, another courier delivers it straight to the recipient's hands — all in under 6 hours.

Because buses are already running on fixed timetables with paid fuel, our network eliminates dedicated long-haul truck overhead. Fares start at just ₹60 for terminal hub drops and from ₹120 for full door-to-door multi-modal delivery. That represents up to 50% savings over conventional next-day air and express courier tariffs.

Yes! Our spatial telematics engine streams live GPS pings from the vehicle carrying your cargo. Go to the Tracking Console and enter your tracking code (e.g. TRK-88219). You will see the vehicle moving along the highway in real time, current highway speed, toll plaza checkpoints, and accurate live ETA down to the minute.

Unlike paper tags that can be peeled off or replaced, every Transitly booking generates an HMAC-SHA256 authenticated digital QR seal. The seal is scanned when loaded into the vehicle cargo bay, validated at intermediate stops, and rescanned before release. If any custody violation or seal tampering occurs, the package is immediately quarantined and our Operations Command Center is alerted.

Dual-OTP guarantees that only the verified consignee can receive the parcel. A secure 6-digit code is dispatched via SMS and WhatsApp to the recipient's phone upon dispatch. The delivery courier cannot complete the handover until the recipient provides this code, which is verified cryptographically against our server.

Not unless you want to! With our Full Door-to-Door option, an on-demand courier partner (Uber Direct or Rapido) will pick up the parcel from your home or office address and transport it to the departure bus terminal. If you prefer to save money, you can also drop it off directly at any designated ISBT Transitly parcel counter.

Because cargo travels on passenger transit buses, passenger safety is paramount. Prohibited items include fireworks, flammable gases and liquids, hazardous chemicals, firearms, unauthorized narcotics, live animals, fragile raw perishables without cold packaging, and currency. Full details are outlined in our Terms of Use.

You can cancel free of charge for a 100% full refund before a first-mile courier is dispatched. If cancelled after a courier has arrived at the sender location, a nominal ₹50 pickup fee is deducted. Once a bus has departed with your parcel in transit, cancellations can no longer be processed. Refunds are credited in 3–5 business days.

The standard weight limit for individual consignments on passenger buses is 30 kg. For higher volume commercial freight or multi-box consignments, please contact our fleet operations team to book dedicated multi-bay cargo allocation.
