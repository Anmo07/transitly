Acceptance of Terms & Scope of Service

These Terms and Conditions of Use ("Terms") constitute a legally binding agreement between you (whether as a Consignor/Sender, Consignee/Recipient, or Transport Operator) and Transitly Logistics Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Transitly").

Transitly operates an orchestration platform that pairs under-utilized cargo bay volume in scheduled public transport vehicles (including state roadways buses such as Haryana Roadways) with door-to-door courier networks (such as Uber Direct, Rapido, and local terminal agents).

By creating a booking, generating a consignment note, or accepting an inbound delivery via OTP, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age and possess full legal capacity to enter into this contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

Parcel Booking & Dynamic Tariffs

Fares on Transitly are calculated algorithmically based on volumetric weight, intercity highway corridor distance, and multi-modal leg feasibility:

  • Volumetric Weight Rule: Fares apply to the greater of dead weight (in kg) or volumetric weight calculated as $(L \times W \times H) / 5000$ (in cm). Maximum single-parcel weight is capped at 30 kg for bus cargo bays.
  • Quote Validity: Rate feasibility quotes remain guaranteed for 15 minutes after generation.
  • Weight Discrepancy: Parcels audited at terminal scale weighing in excess of stated booking weight are subject to supplementary surcharge prior to dispatch.

Chain of Custody & Dual-OTP Protocols

Every shipment processed through Transitly is governed by strict tamper-evident custody requirements:

qr_code_2
Cryptographic QR Bay Seals A digital seal is generated upon pickup and scanned at every handover (bus boarding, terminal sorting, and courier dispatch). Any broken or mismatched seal triggers immediate telemetry quarantine.
pin
Mandatory Recipient OTP Delivery Final handover to consignee requires verbal confirmation of a cryptographically generated 6-digit OTP dispatched to recipient’s verified mobile number. Consignment is legally delivered only when OTP is verified.
5

First-Mile & Last-Mile Partner Operations

Where door-to-door delivery is booked, the first-mile pickup and last-mile delivery legs are dispatched via integrated courier partners (such as Uber Direct, Rapido, or certified local riders).

If severe weather, roadblocks, or rider unserviceability prevent doorstep delivery, Transitly will alert consignee and provide safe self-pickup options from the nearest destination ISBT terminal counter with a pro-rata refund of the last-mile delivery fee.

6

Carrier Liability, Transit Cover & Claims

Transitly provides standard complimentary transit protection coverage up to ₹2,000 per consignment for loss or irreparable physical damage resulting directly from vehicle accident or gross negligence.

Claim Notification Window: Consignee must file claims within 48 hours of package handover or scheduled ETA via the Help & Support Console with unboxing photographs.

Exclusions from Liability: Transitly is not liable for indirect or consequential losses, electronic data loss, mechanical failure of unpadded electronics, perishable goods spoilage, or delays resulting from Force Majeure events (floods, fog, highway strikes, state bandhs).

7

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancelled before first-mile rider dispatch: 100% Full Refund (Zero Fee)
Cancelled after rider arrived at sender location: Refund less ₹50 pickup cancellation fee
Cancelled after package stowed in bus cargo bay: Non-refundable (Bus in Transit)

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source within 3-5 business days via UPI or card network.
