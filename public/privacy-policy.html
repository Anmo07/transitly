Overview & Scope

Transitly Logistics Technologies ("Transitly", "we", "us", or "our") operates a multi-modal intercity public transport parcel monetization platform. This Privacy Policy details our commitment to transparency regarding the collection, transmission, storage, and processing of personal data when you use our web platform, mobile applications, or partner logistics interfaces.

By booking cargo, tracking consignments, verifying OTPs, or utilizing our door-to-door courier connections, you consent to the data practices described herein. This policy is aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and international data protection standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2

Information We Collect

To deliver parcels securely across bus corridors and doorstep partners, we collect specific categories of data:

badge User & Contact Information

Sender and recipient legal names, verified E.164 phone numbers, delivery postal addresses, landmark coordinates, and optional email addresses for electronic airway bills (e-AWB).

inventory_2 Consignment & Cargo Data

Parcel weight, dimensions, packaging type, stated category, declared valuation, and affirmative declarations verifying that shipments contain no hazardous or prohibited contraband.

lock Security & Custody Verification Tokens

Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 digital bay seals, One-Time Password (OTP) verification records, driver handover logs, and timestamped geofence validation coordinates.

3

Telematics & Real-Time Spatial GPS Data

Transitly’s core value is providing transparent, sub-second visibility into intercity transit vehicles carrying your parcel.

  • Transit Fleet GPS: We ingest automated vehicle telemetry (latitude, longitude, speed, heading, and corridor progress) from onboard bus IoT devices and driver terminals to compute real-time Estimated Times of Arrival (ETA).
  • Customer Geolocation: With your explicit browser permission, we access your device GPS to suggest nearest ISBT hubs and calculate first-mile pickup feasibility. You may disable this at any time in your device settings.
  • Spatial Geofencing: We create virtual circular and polygonal boundaries around authorized bus terminals (e.g. Kashmiri Gate, Chandigarh ISBT 17) to verify that cargo transfers take place exclusively within verified custody zones.
4

How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data strictly for operational, security, and statutory compliance reasons:

Route & Capacity Matching Assigning parcels to scheduled public transport buses with available cargo bay volume.
Chain of Custody Validation Ensuring cargo is never released without authorized QR seal validation and recipient OTP verification.
Dispatch Notifications Transmitting milestone updates via SMS, WhatsApp Cloud API, and push alerts.
Financial Reconciliation Settling capacity payments with state roadways operators and last-mile dispatch couriers.
5

Partners & Third-Party Sharing

Transitly does not sell or rent personal information to brokers or advertisers. Data is shared exclusively with operational partners necessary to fulfill multi-modal delivery:

  • directions_bus State & Public Transport Operators: (e.g. Haryana Roadways, DTC, PRTC) receive consignment tracking IDs, bay assignment tags, and weight data for bus stowage.
  • two_wheeler Last-Mile Couriers: (e.g. Uber Direct, Rapido) receive sender/recipient addresses and contact numbers to complete doorstep pickups and final deliveries.
  • chat Communication Gateways: Meta WhatsApp Cloud API and transactional SMS providers receive phone numbers to deliver OTPs and live tracking links.
6

Security Architecture & Data Retention

Transitly implements defense-in-depth technical safeguards to preserve confidentiality and chain-of-custody integrity:

TLS 1.3 & AES-256 All telematics, REST endpoints, and WebSockets are encrypted in transit and at rest.
Hashed Passwords & OTPs Raw OTPs are never stored plain-text; they are verified via salted cryptographic digests.
FIDO2 / WebAuthn Administrative command center access requires hardware/biometric security keys.

Retention Policy: Spatial GPS telemetry pings are kept in fast Redis streams for live tracking, consolidated into PostGIS geospatial archives, and pruned after 180 days. Immutable transaction logs (airway bills, tax invoices, and chain-of-custody handovers) are retained for 7 years to satisfy commercial logistics and taxation regulations.

Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request a summary of personal data processed by Transitly and processing activities undertaken.
  • Request correction or completion of inaccurate or misleading personal data.
  • Request erasure of your account and personal data, subject to legal record retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. texting STOP on WhatsApp to cease promotional alerts).
  • Nominate an individual to exercise privacy rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential cookies to maintain user authentication sessions, localized storage to remember your preferred corridor searches and selected UI language, and privacy-preserving telemetry cookies. You can inspect or modify your choices at any time using our floating cookie preferences banner or via App Settings.
